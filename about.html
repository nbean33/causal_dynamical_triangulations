Credits

The work described on this site builds on the following codes.

CodeAuthors
2+1 and 3+1 dimensional CDT in Common Lisp, the basis of the 4D audit and portRajesh Kommu
Hořava–Lifshitz variant of the Lisp codeChristian Anderson
Fixed and open boundary conditions for the 2+1 codeDavid Kamensky; merged into the main code by Jonah Miller
Sphere generator and documentationJonah Miller and Joshua Cooperman
2d-cdt, the basis of the 2D code and of the shared headersJoren Brunekreef and Andrzej Görlich
3d-cdt, the basis of the 3D codeJoren Brunekreef, Daniel Németh and Andrzej Görlich

The design of the C++ codebase follows Brunekreef, Görlich and Loll, Simulating CDT quantum gravity (2310.16744). The physics targets used for validation are taken from the papers of Jan Ambjørn, Andrzej Görlich, Jerzy Jurkiewicz and Renate Loll, and the curvature comparisons from those of Joren Brunekreef, Nora Klitgaard and Renate Loll. The full list is given on the references page.

Technical notes

The site consists of static HTML, CSS and JavaScript without a build step and is hosted on GitHub Pages. Results and figures are copied from the research repository by a script that records the source commit. Equations are rendered with KaTeX, and the interactive components run entirely in the browser.
