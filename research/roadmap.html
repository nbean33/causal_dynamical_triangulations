In progress

  • 4D curvature profile of the de Sitter universe. The observable has been implemented and tested; production runs are in progress.
  • Bookkeeping versus blind guessing. Paired chains in 3D and 4D compare equilibrium means, autocorrelation times and the wall-clock time per independent sample. A higher acceptance rate does not by itself imply faster sampling; the benchmark will determine whether bookkeeping should be extended.
  • 3D spatial slices, following arXiv:2208.12718.

Open questions

  • A fully equilibrated de Sitter universe at large volume. At \(N_{41}\) = 20k and 40k a secondary lump persists; at 80k no single blob formed in \(3\times10^{10}\) proposals per chain. Growing the volume from a narrow start has not succeeded; a more promising starting point is a blob of the expected width, for example a rescaled equilibrated 40k configuration.
  • The Hausdorff dimension \(D_H\), which requires equilibrated profiles at two or more volumes.
  • The spectral dimension at \(N_4 \approx 180\text{k}\), the volume of the published fit, which has the same requirement.

Extensions suggested by the methods paper

The methods paper of the 2d-cdt and 3d-cdt codes (2310.16744) suggests several extensions. Each is rated below by its risk to the correctness of the sampler and by the effort required:

The planned order begins with the least costly and lowest-risk items, each of which makes the next measurable: completion of the benchmark, the thermalisation check and the loop escape, extension of the benchmark to 2D, and then a decision on full bookkeeping based on the results.

GPU-parallel moves are not planned. The moves are local but sequential; a parallel chain would require a new proof of detailed balance and could not be verified with the existing exact tests. Running many independent chains already makes full use of the hardware without any risk to correctness.
